The Four Archival Pillars of Conservation

Conservation framing is not merely an aesthetic choice; it is an environmental safeguard that shields delicate organic papers, canvases, and inks against decomposition.

99% UV Intercept

OptiView UV Glass

Blocks 99% of ultraviolet radiation that triggers irreversible molecular fading in watercolors, pencil sketches, and photographic dyes.

Made from virgin cotton linters completely free of acidic lignin and wood pulp. Buffered with 3% calcium carbonate to neutralize ambient pollutants.

Studio Methodology

The Four-Step Framing Journey

From your initial consultation to final inspection, our structured workflow ensures precision engineering and museum-level preservation at every stage.

1

Curatorial Consultation

We review your artwork's medium, historical era, physical condition, and lighting environment to recommend compatible moulding styles and mat proportions.

2

Archival Specification

We match 100% cotton rag mats, select appropriate UV-filtering glazing, and formulate non-destructive reversible hinging strategies tailored to the paper weight.

3

Handcrafted Joinery

Hardwood mouldings are hand-mitred, corner-splined, and clamped on our precision assembly table. Mat apertures are bevel-cut with micrometric accuracy.

4

Fitting & Final Seal

Assembled in a static-free chamber, backed with an impenetrable vapor seal, fitted with heavy-duty hanging hardware, and certified with our Studio Seal.
