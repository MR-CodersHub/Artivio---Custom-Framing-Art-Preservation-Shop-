Paper Conservation

Certificates, Diplomas & Historic Documents

Parchment, vintage maps, deeds, and academic diplomas require careful microclimates to counteract brittleness, acidic yellowing, and humidity swings.

Archival Encapsulation & De-Acidification

Early 20th-century wood pulp papers contain high concentrations of residual alum rosin that create self-destructive acid burning. Our certified paper preservationists employ non-invasive encapsulation techniques:

  • ✔ Mylar Polyester Encapsulation: Electrostatic inert pocketing that secures fragile, torn documents without adhesive contact.
  • ✔ Micro-Chamber Backing: Molecular sieve paper containing activated zeolites that capture airborne pollutants and organic degradation byproducts.
  • ✔ Embossed Seal Cutouts: Precision multi-aperture matboards that frame both the primary document and gold-foil wax seals seamlessly.

Artisan Restoration

Antique Frame Restoration & 22k Gold Gilding

Historic frames are precious works of art in their own right. We restore cracked plaster ornaments, reconstruct missing corners, and re-gild patinas.

Phase 01

Plaster & Composition Casting

We take dental-grade silicone impressions of intact sections on your antique frame to cast exact replica flourishes, replacing lost corners and cracked relief bands.

  • Exact replica silicone impression casting
  • Historic animal hide & gypsum compound
  • Hand-carved invisible seam blending
Phase 02

22k Water & Oil Re-Gilding

Application of genuine 22k, 23.75k gold, silver, and palladium leaf. We match the historical clay bole undercoat so natural wear reveals genuine antique character.

  • Armenian red & yellow clay bole underlayer
  • Genuine 22k European gold leaf application
  • Natural agate gemstone burnishing polish
Phase 03

Corner Joint Re-Splining

Heavy antique frames often pull apart under glass weight. We disassemble loose miters, remove brittle hide glue, insert internal hardwood biscuits, and clamp square.

  • Brittle 19th-century glue de-bonding
  • Internal walnut spline reinforcement
  • Precision pneumatic square clamping
