Archival Integrity

Museum-Grade Conservation & Preservation

Ordinary framing materials release acidic gases and allow harmful ultraviolet rays to yellow paper and fade pigments. At Artivio, every component is rigorously tested to museum conservation standards.

01

Tru Vue® Museum Glass (99% UV Blocking)

Virtually invisible anti-reflective coating reduces glare to under 1% while filtering out 99% of damaging UV light wavelengths.

02

100% Cotton Rag Matboards & Mounts

Naturally lignin-free and buffered with calcium carbonate to actively neutralize environmental atmospheric pollutants.

03

Reversible Archival Hinging

We use Japanese mulberry paper and cooked wheat starch paste. Art is never taped or permanently glued—all mounting is 100% reversible.

04

Hermetic Microclimate Dust Backing

Archival vapor barrier and acid-free moisture seals protect against humidity swings, insect intrusion, and ambient airborne dust.
